Frequently asked questions

A few details before you request a booking.

Which areas do you serve?

NeatNest is starting in Greater Noida. Share your sector or society in the booking form so we can check whether a provider is available in your location.

Are the prices fixed?

The prices shown are starting estimates. The final price depends on the property, condition, requested tasks and availability. We will confirm the quote and inclusions before accepting the booking.

Is my booking confirmed when I submit the form?

No. The form opens WhatsApp with your request. Your booking is confirmed only after NeatNest replies and agrees on the service, price and time.

Do I pay on this website?

No. This website does not collect payments. Confirm the payment method and any advance amount directly with NeatNest before the visit.

What if I need to cancel or change the time?

Message NeatNest as soon as possible. Cancellation, rescheduling and refund terms will be shared during booking confirmation.
